Credibility & Recognition

The 3 Verified Onnoy Badges

Each badge represents an escalation in skill — from foundational knowledge to hands-on action and community leadership. Badges are tied to your unique Onnoy ID and can be featured on your academic profile, resume, and social media.

Tier 1 · Knowledge Foundation

Informed

অবহিত নাগরিক

Certifies that a student understands digital footprint dynamics, online attention algorithms, phishing scams, and ethical AI practices.

Earning Criteria:

  • Complete all 5 Level 1 Digital Citizenship modules (Attention Literacy, Misinformation, Scam Safety, AI Integrity, Digital Footprint).
  • Pass all interactive knowledge checkpoints with at least 80% accuracy.
  • Complete the baseline self-assessment reflection.

Tier 2 · Practical Action

Aware

সচেতন ডিফেন্ডার

Certifies active application of critical judgment in the real world: debunking misinformation, running privacy audits, and building healthier digital habits.

Earning Criteria:

  • Must have already earned the Tier 1: Informed Badge.
  • Successfully complete and submit 3 Level 2 real-world missions:
    — Fact-check a viral social claim with 2+ trusted sources.
    — Conduct a family/friend screen audit & digital agreement.
    — Audit an AI tool for hallucinations or bias.
  • Submission reviewed and approved by the Onnoy mentor team.
Explore Level 2 Missions →
Tier 3 · Community Leadership

Guardian

ডিজিটাল অভিভাবক ও লিডার

The premier honor for student ambassadors who advocate for digital ethics, guide peers against scams, and foster independent thinking across Bangladesh.

Earning Criteria:

  • Must have earned both Informed and Aware badges.
  • Complete all 4 Level 2 Missions with exemplary performance.
  • Lead an awareness discussion or share an Onnoy workbook in your school, college, or community group.
  • Direct approval and verification by Onnoy Founders and Academic Council.
