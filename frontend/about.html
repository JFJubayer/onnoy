About Onnoy

Onnoy — Connecting necessary dots

WHO ARE WE?

Onnoy - অন্বয় is a youth-led initiative dedicated to Developing Independent Thinkers for Bangladesh. We equip secondary and higher-secondary students (Class 9–12), parents, and teachers with practical decision-making workbooks, interactive video courses, school sessions, and mentorship.

Growing up digital in Bangladesh brings intense pressure — from conflicting career advice and fear of AI eliminating jobs to endless doom-scrolling and family screen-time battles. We believe compliance and restriction aren't enough; young people need independent judgement to think critically, choose wisely, and navigate technology on their own terms.

"Don't just hand someone an answer. Hand them a way to think that holds up whatever the outcome."

WHAT WE DO

Decision Workbooks

Structured guides like Not Sure What to Study? and Learn What Lasts to help students choose higher education fields and future-proof skills calmly.

Interactive AI Courses

Two video courses with timestamped checkpoint questions, verified digital certificates, and the Onnoy Timesave browser extension to reclaim focus.

Sessions & Mentorship

Interactive school workshops across Mymensingh, reusable kits for teachers, and plain-language screen-time guides for parents to foster family dialogue.

OUR FRAMEWORK

Our curriculum combines the UNESCO Digital Competency Framework and ISTE Digital Citizenship Standards with our 3 Decision Pillars: (1) What to Study & Career Choices, (2) AI & Technology on Your Terms, and (3) Screen Time & Family Well-being.

HOW WE STAY INDEPENDENT

☕ Grassroots & Community Powered

Keeping Truth & Education Free from Commercial Influence

Onnoy is 100% volunteer-powered by young graduates, technologists, and educators in Mymensingh. We do not sell ads, accept corporate sponsorships that compromise our principles, or charge students for workshops and workbooks. Micro-donations via ChaBondhu allow everyday citizens to treat our team to a cup of tea and directly fund printed learning materials for adolescents.
